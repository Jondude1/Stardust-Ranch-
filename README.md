# Stardust Ranch

A farming and creature-collecting game for Android. Run your grandmother's old ranch, grow crops through the seasons, explore the forest, beach, mountain and a 100-floor mine, and catch original creatures that help work your farm.

All art (tiles, buildings, people, crops, items and all 36 creatures) is drawn in code — no outside assets.

## What's in it
- **Open world:** Farm, Town, Forest, Beach, Mountain and the Mines, plus house, store and creature center interiors
- **Farming:** till, plant, water, harvest; 13 crops across 4 seasons, some keep producing; rain, snow, sprinklers, shipping bin
- **Gathering:** chop trees, break rocks, cut weeds, daily seasonal forage, beach shells
- **Mines:** procedural floors, copper/iron/gold/gems/coal, hidden ladders, gloops to fight with your sword, treasure chests every 10 floors, mine cart every 5, something legendary at floor 100
- **Creatures:** 36 original species with types, moves, leveling and evolution; turn-based battles; catch them with Capture Orbs
- **Helpers:** every creature has a farm job (Waterer, Gardener, Clearer, Digger, Forager, Harvester, Lucky). Your lead creature follows you and boosts your tools; ranch creatures work the farm every morning and drop finds in the Ranch Box
- **Town:** shop, blacksmith tool upgrades, crafting, 5 villagers with friendship and gifts
- **Self-updating:** checks this repo's latest release and installs new versions in-app

## Controls
Drag on the left side to walk. Green button = use tool / talk / interact (hold to keep swinging). Menu button for items, party, crafting, creature log, friends and options. Tap hotbar slots to pick items; the number next to the hotbar switches rows.

## Building
Every push to `main` builds a signed APK in GitHub Actions and publishes it as a release.
